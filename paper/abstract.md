# Abstract

7GC Digital Twin: A Community-Scale Evaluation Framework for Trustworthy AI-Native 6G Connectivity presents a **research prototype** artifact for community-scale 6G research. Contribution focus: digital-twin schema, Gary flagship node, 7GC scenario design, digital equality metrics, export contract.
Results reported here are from **preliminary toy demonstrations** using synthetic data only; field validation is future work.
We do not claim operational 6G deployment, Oulu/CWC affiliation, or carrier-grade performance.
