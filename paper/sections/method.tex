\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
The frozen protocol is \texttt{paper/artifacts/experiment\_protocol.yaml}, mirroring \texttt{configs/experiments/rq1\_gary\_flagship\_profiles.yaml}.
Seeds are $1,2,7,42$. Mode is \texttt{synthetic-fixture}.
Requested metric families are workload, compute, radio, failure, mobility, and inclusion.

\paragraph{Continuity levels.}
Levels follow the Device OS rule (re-implemented in this repo and checked against stored labels):
\texttt{connected}$\rightarrow$\texttt{target};
\texttt{degraded} or \texttt{transitioning}$\rightarrow$\texttt{degraded};
\texttt{offline} and the workload is in \texttt{offline\_capabilities}$\rightarrow$\texttt{min\_useful};
otherwise \texttt{failed} (below min-useful).
The injected bearer corpus is the same labeled set used by Device OS tests (\texttt{wifi\_nominal}, \texttt{dock\_ethernet}, \texttt{degraded\_wifi}, \texttt{jam\_wifi\_offline\_fallback}, \texttt{force\_offline}, plus an explicit wearable coding path).
Those bearer metrics are scenario \emph{inputs}, not channel soundings~\cite{itur_m2160}.

\paragraph{Gary overlay.}
Gary bad-day names are mapped to bearer-stress labels \emph{before} looking at outcomes (protocol field \texttt{gary\_scenario\_to\_bearer\_stress}):
neighborhood outage $\rightarrow$ jam/offline fallback;
storm congestion, school bandwidth collapse, and library hotspot overload $\rightarrow$ degraded Wi-Fi;
SMB payment down $\rightarrow$ force offline.
The mapping is an assumption, not a field measurement.

\paragraph{Seeds and sites.}
Synthetic users are drawn with \texttt{generate\_users(n, seed)} so demand and mobility can vary across the pre-registered seeds.
Radio $\mathrm{SINR}$ remains a YAML stub (\texttt{sinr\_db\_stub}) and is not seed-jittered.
Comparative sites are scored with the same generator; inclusion composites hash site identifiers and barrier lists and are not RF.

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
No University of Oulu affiliation, no community deployment, no held-out RF campaign, no SUBMITTED/ACCEPTED status.
Baselines named for later campaigns (terrestrial-only, cloud-only, local-only, oracle) are not executed in this digital run.
