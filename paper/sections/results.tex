The only below-min-useful row in the frozen corpus is wearable $\times$ \texttt{offline\_coding} $\times$ \texttt{force\_offline\_coding\_unsupported}.
Desk, mobile/docked, and local-creation never reach \texttt{failed}: when forced offline they remain at \texttt{min\_useful} because their primary workloads are listed in \texttt{offline\_capabilities}.
Wearable offline coverage includes lessons and games but not coding, writing, or research notebooks, which is why the explicit coding path fails.

\paragraph{Labeled degrade that does not degrade.}
All four classes still score \texttt{target} on the scenario named \texttt{degraded\_wifi} (orchestrator state remains \texttt{connected} in this injected corpus).
The benchmark therefore does \emph{not} treat that label as evidence of a min-useful miss.
That is a property of the current digital corpus, not a measured Wi-Fi fade.
