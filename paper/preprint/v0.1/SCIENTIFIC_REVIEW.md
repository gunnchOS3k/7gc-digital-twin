# Paper I skeptical scientific review — 13 October 2026

Decision for the bounded exploratory preprint: **READY FOR OWNER REVIEW, conditional on the package acceptance record**. Human scientific approval and originality verification remain false. This is an AI-assisted, separately implemented computational audit, not an independent external human review or a journal acceptance decision.

The implemented question is what a stored state/workload-support classifier and a separate fixed-prior seeded generator establish about their own evidence semantics. It does not ask whether a physical device meets a validated service threshold or whether a new controller outperforms an executed baseline. The contribution is methodological: a small reproducible corpus, exact source/output reconciliation, analytic characterization and identification of misleading metric interpretations. Software functionality and elementary expectations alone do not establish new general scientific knowledge.

## Mathematical and statistical audit

Eq.(1) is a categorical decision rule, not cardinal utility; averaging its codes would require a separate assumption. Eq.(2) probabilities stay within [0,1] at all configured priors; Eq.(3) completion is gated by useful occupancy and uses a task draw only when useful. Preserve the class order, unused demand draw and conditional RNG draw to reproduce exact rows. Eq.(4) counts fractions over 48 abstract steps per class; steps have no physical duration. Eqs.(5–6) use n=30 sample SD (ddof=1) and t(29)=2.0452296421. Eqs.(7–8) integrate the fixed uniform-stress model, independently checked by quadrature. They are post hoc characterization, not new data.

## Findings and limits

Favorable: eight declared supported offline rows retain the minimum-useful label; implementation parity is reproducible. Negative: unsupported wearable coding fails; degraded-state stress coverage is absent; several apparently independent endpoints duplicate a Boolean sequence; no successful-restoration measurement exists. Inconclusive: application utility, physical continuity, common device ranking, fair controller effects, transferable novelty and journal significance. The separate random generator does not validate the deterministic corpus.

No new sweep, seed expansion, retuning, physical collection or baseline execution was performed. A small existing 30-seed run is used only to validate clean archive portability. Historical raw results, frozen protocol and journal copy are preserved.
