# Novelty assessment

The manuscript’s Table 1 is the source-specific novelty matrix, based on the inspection levels recorded in CITATION_VERIFICATION.md. Sterbenz establishes service resilience, Satyanarayanan edge locality, EdgeDroid actual task/trace benchmarking, Fuller physical/virtual integration, and Li a native network twin. This paper adds an executable small declared-workload corpus plus an audit of the current generator and metric semantics. No priority, optimal-policy or calibrated device-performance claim is made. The deterministic classifier and elementary expectation formulas alone may be insufficient journal novelty. Owner and independent specialist judgment remain pending; missing physical tests and fair controller comparisons are stated in the manuscript.
