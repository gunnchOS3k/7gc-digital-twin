\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
IMT-2030 usage scenarios treat connectivity as a service with reliability, latency, and availability targets, not merely a radio-on indicator~\cite{itur_m2160,3gpp_ts22261}.
On resource-constrained devices the gap is sharper: an orchestrator can report a bearer while the workload the human needed (offline lessons, local coding, sensing) is unsupported.
Paper~I asks which of four research classes remain at or above min-useful service under a frozen digital corpus, and how those labels overlay onto Gary bad-day names.

The primary artifacts are this digital twin's experiment \texttt{rq1\_gary\_flagship\_profiles} and a frozen snapshot of Device OS service-continuity profiles (\texttt{fixtures/device\_os/SERVICE\_CONTINUITY\_PROFILES.json}, content digest recorded in the protocol).
Gary is the flagship \emph{research scenario}, not a deployed community network.
Comparative 7GC names are scenario environments used for inclusion-family contrast; they are not community deployments.

The contribution is a pre-registered, seed-logged benchmark that \emph{reports failure cases}.
Building the harness is not the claim. The claim, bounded to \texttt{SYNTHETIC\_SIM}, is the pattern the harness returns when run.
