# 7GC Scenario Framework

## Framing

7GC is a distributed living-lab and digital-twin scenario framework, not a promise to build seven campuses during the PhD.

The dissertation will research the 6G service-continuity architecture, simulation framework, digital-twin validation model, and prototype workload requirements needed to make this ecosystem technically feasible. 7GC provides the scenario diversity that makes the evaluation rigorous and reproducible.

## Scenario Mapping

| 7GC node | Research scenario class | Minimum status before application |
|----------|------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Gary | Primary proof context: digital equity, schools, libraries, civic spaces | Most developed |
| Ghana | Mobile-first learning, low-bandwidth inclusion, youth opportunity | Scenario brief |
| Guyana | Coastal resilience, river/interior connectivity, climate/geospatial data | Scenario brief |

| Geelong | Applied industry, manufacturing, port/logistics, design | Scenario brief |
| Germany | Apprenticeship systems, industrial cybersecurity, Industry 4.0 | Scenario brief |
| Graham Land | Extreme connectivity, satellite/NTN, polar digital twin | Simulation-only / partner-gated |

## Methodology

Digital twins serve as:
1. **Simulation environments** — parameterized models of network conditions
2. **Scenario generators** — diverse connectivity conditions for evaluation
3. **Reproducibility tools** — identical conditions across experiment runs
4. **Planning instruments** — "what-if" scenarios without field deployment

Digital twins do NOT:
- Prove real-world impact (until calibrated with field measurements)
- Replace field validation
- Guarantee modeled conditions match reality
- Substitute for community partnership and governance

## Non-negotiables

- Do not say seven campuses will be built during the PhD
- Do not imply field access where none exists
- Do not treat the seven nodes as equal field sites
- Do not use Gaza or Graham Land as branding
- Do not collect data from vulnerable communities without governance
- Do not claim digital twins prove impact
- Gary is primary; others are scenario classes unless partners/permissions exist
